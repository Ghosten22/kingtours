Rum, chocolate and waterfall

The short version of Grenada, and the one most guests book first — especially off a cruise ship or on a half-free day.

Cocoa goes from pod to bar in front of you: fermentation, nibs, butter, and why lightly processed chocolate tastes nothing like the supermarket kind. Then a distillery where the machinery predates the engine. Annandale Falls closes it out, close enough to the road that nobody has to hike.

Stops

  • House of Chocolate or Diamond Chocolate Factory
  • A working rum distillery, with tasting
  • Annandale Falls — swim stop
  • Hotel, villa or cruise pier pickup and drop-off

Bring swimwear, a towel, and small cash for site entry and the falls guides. Cold water is on board; guests often mention beers and rum punch too. Also bookable upfront on GetYourGuide.

The full island day

Rum, chocolate, spice and Annandale in a single loop, with the mountains and the north coast added on the way. Long day, but you finish it actually knowing where you have been.

North from Grand Anse through St. George’s and up the Caribbean side, over the top through Grand Etang, across to St. Patrick’s, then back down the Atlantic coast. Lunch happens somewhere along the way — Belmont Estate for the full plantation version, or a local spot if you would rather.

Stops

  • Fort Frederick, above St. George’s
  • Grand Etang crater lake and the mona monkeys
  • Belmont Estate cocoa plantation
  • A spice station — nutmeg, mace, cinnamon
  • Sauteurs, Leapers’ Hill and Levera
  • River Antoine or Clarke’s Court rum

Site fees and lunch are paid separately. Comfortable shoes and sun cover. Best started early — the morning light on the east coast is worth the alarm.

Waterfalls and rainforest

For people who came to get in the water. Three or four falls, chosen on the day based on rain, road conditions and how much walking the group is up for.

Annandale is the easy one, a few steps from the car park. Concord takes a little more. Seven Sisters and Mt. Carmel involve a proper walk and are the ones people remember. Start early enough and you can have a pool entirely to yourself.

Stops

  • Annandale Falls
  • Concord Falls
  • Seven Sisters or Mt. Carmel
  • Grand Etang National Park
  • Laura Herb and Spice Garden, if there is time

Shoes with grip that you do not mind soaking, a dry change of clothes, and small cash for entry and trail guides.

Build your own day

This is how most repeat guests book. Send a list of what you want to see and the order, timing and pickup get worked out around it — usually with two or three stops you would not have known to ask for.

Detours mid-tour are fine. People have added Welcome Stone for the view, stopped at a beach that looked good from the road, been driven to and from a dive shop across a week, and had a restaurant table booked from the car for that evening. One proposal has happened on a tour, camera ready.

Stops

  • Underwater Sculpture Park and snorkelling
  • Laura Herb and Spice Garden
  • Welcome Stone and the southern ridges
  • Saturday market in St. George’s
  • Dive shop or marina shuttles across your stay
  • Photo stops, proposals, anniversaries

Travelling with children is fine — there is room for twelve in the van and drivers have been known to put the kids’ music on.
