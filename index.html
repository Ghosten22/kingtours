The people driving.

Joel runs most trips himself. When he is already out, one of his own drivers takes over — and you will know who is coming before they arrive.

Joel Francis

Owner · “King J”

Quickest to reach on WhatsApp, and the one guests come back to. Known for building tours around a list rather than a fixed route, booking restaurant tables from the car, and checking in the day after you fly home.

Deshon

The one reviewers ask for by name. Runs waterfall and distillery days at a pace that suits the group, knows the angles for photographs, and keeps up a running commentary on cocoa, nutmeg and island history.
